03 / First response

Know your next move.

A legal reference should help a person act. These are general safety steps, not legal advice.

01

If you’re the victim

Preserve evidence before you block, delete, or reset anything.

  • Capture URLs, usernames, timestamps, and transaction IDs.
  • Secure email first, then change reused passwords and enable MFA.
  • Report to your platform, bank, and local cybercrime portal.
02

If you’re researching

Keep claims traceable and current.

  • Prefer gazettes, regulator pages, and official legislation databases.
  • Record the law’s date, status, and whether it is in force.
  • Use AI to summarize — have a human verify every citation.
